Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In short: KinPin Studio runs on one person's own computer, keeps its data there, and sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.

Who this covers

This policy covers the KinPin Studio Windows app and this website. The app is made and used by one person, Dean (the developer), for his own Pinterest account and his own Amazon Associates account. There is no service run for other users and no sign-up.

This website

  • It has no cookies, no analytics, no forms, no ads and no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep technical records of visits, such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The developer does not see or use them.

The app: what it connects to

The app only talks to Pinterest and Amazon, and only with the owner's own permission. It has no server of its own.

Pinterest

The owner signs in with Pinterest and approves read-only access. The app asks for these permissions:

Permission What the app uses it for
boards:read Read the names of the owner's boards.
pins:read Read the owner's pins (title, description, link, board, date, type) and their numbers: views, saves and clicks per day.
user_accounts:read Read the account's username, to show which account is connected, and Pinterest's public trending keywords, which are not about any person.

The app never creates, edits or deletes anything on Pinterest. It reads data about the owner's own account only, and it does not collect information about other Pinterest users.

Amazon

  • The app asks Amazon's Creators API for product information on amazon.de: title, price, picture, availability and sales rank. This is public product information.
  • It does not receive or store any information about Amazon customers.
  • Amazon Associates earnings reports are downloaded by the owner and placed in a folder on the computer. The app reads these files on the computer. They list products, quantities and earnings.

Where the data is kept

  • Everything is stored on the owner's computer, in a database in the app's data folder (in the Windows user profile).
  • Passwords and access tokens (the keys that let the app talk to Pinterest and Amazon) are encrypted with Windows secure storage. They are never shown again and never written to log files.
  • The app keeps its own history of the numbers, because Pinterest only provides the last 90 days. This history is how it can show what works over time.

Who gets the data

Nobody. The data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. The app contains no analytics or tracking, and it does not send data to the developer. The only network requests it makes are to Pinterest and Amazon, to read the data described above.

Deleting your data

  • Disconnect Pinterest: in the app, open Settings, then Connections, and choose Disconnect. This deletes the stored Pinterest access.
  • Remove the app's permission: in your Pinterest account settings, remove KinPin Studio from the apps that have access.
  • Delete everything stored: delete the app's data folder in the Windows user profile. Uninstalling the app does not delete this folder by itself.

Your rights

The app only handles the owner's own account data on the owner's own computer, and nothing reaches the developer. If you think this website or the app holds information about you, or you have any question about this policy, write to the contact address below. You also have the usual rights under data protection law, such as access, correction and deletion.

Children

The app and website are not meant for children and do not knowingly collect their data.
