KinPin Studio

A private desktop app that brings Pinterest and Amazon numbers into one place, so a pin creator can see what is trending, what to pin next and what is working.

What it is

KinPin Studio is a Windows app made for one person: its developer, who runs a Pinterest account that links to products on amazon.de through the Amazon Associates programme. It runs on that person's own computer. It is not a public service, and there are no accounts or sign-ups.

Pinterest and Amazon already show their own numbers. This app gathers them in one place and turns them into highlights and suggestions.

What it does

  • Trend Radar: keeps a daily record of what is trending on Pinterest, with a calendar of seasonal peaks, so the right time to start pinning is clear.
  • Product Finder: finds amazon.de products that fit a trend and keeps a watchlist.
  • Pins: shows the pins on the Pinterest account with their views, saves and clicks, next to what the linked products earned.
  • Insights: shows which keywords, boards and formats work best, and warns when a pin loses its link or stops getting clicks.
  • Today: a short daily list of what to pin, with a link ready to copy.

What it does not do

  • It does not create, change, publish or delete anything on Pinterest. Access is read-only.
  • It does not post on anyone's behalf and does not message other people.
  • It does not collect data about other Pinterest users or Amazon customers.
  • It does not send any data to the developer, and it has no ads or tracking.

How information is handled is explained in the privacy policy.
